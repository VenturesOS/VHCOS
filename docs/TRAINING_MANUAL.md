# 1. INTRODUCTION

## 1.1 What Is Ventures HRD Talent OS?

Ventures HRD Talent OS is a recruitment management portal designed for industrial hiring. It helps employers post jobs, track candidates through a structured hiring pipeline, and collaborate with recruiters — all from a single dashboard.

## 1.2 Who Is This Training For?

This manual is designed for two roles:

- **Employers** — Team leaders or hiring managers who create jobs, review candidates, and make hiring decisions.
- **Recruiters** — Sourcing professionals who find candidates, submit them to jobs, and move them through pipeline stages.

## 1.3 What You Will Learn

By the end of this training, you will be able to:

- Log in and navigate the portal confidently
- Understand your dashboard and its key sections
- Perform your daily tasks without assistance
- Track candidates through the hiring pipeline
- Use the submission tracker to monitor progress
- Avoid common mistakes that slow down your workflow

## 1.4 What This Training Does NOT Cover

This training focuses on foundational daily operations only. The following advanced features are not covered here and will be addressed in separate training:

- AI-powered candidate screening and matching
- Advanced search and filtering
- Analytics deep-dives
- Automation rules and workflows
- Revenue intelligence
- System administration

# 2. QUICK START GUIDE (15 Minutes)

Use this section when you need to get someone operational immediately.

## 2.1 Logging In

**Steps:**
1. Open your browser and go to the portal URL provided by your administrator.
2. Enter your **email address** in the Email field.
3. Enter your **password** in the Password field.
4. Click the **Login** button.
5. You will be taken to your role-specific dashboard.

**Common Mistake:** Using a personal email instead of the company email assigned to you. Your account is linked to the email your administrator registered.

**Tip:** If you forget your password, use the "Forgot Password" link on the login page. A reset link will be sent to your registered email.

## 2.2 Dashboard Overview

After logging in, you will see your **Dashboard**. This is your home base. Here is what to look for:

| Area | What It Shows |
|------|--------------|
| **Left Sidebar** | Navigation menu — all your portal sections listed here |
| **Main Content Area** | The page you are currently viewing |
| **Top Section** | Your name, role, and account options |

**The sidebar is your map.** Every section of the portal is accessible from it. If you ever feel lost, click **Dashboard** in the sidebar to return home.

## 2.3 Key Navigation Areas

**For Employers** (sidebar items you will use daily):
- **Dashboard** — Overview of your hiring activity
- **My Jobs** — All jobs you have created
- **Post Job** — Create a new job listing
- **Pipeline** — See where candidates are in the hiring process
- **Trackers** — Detailed submission tracking spreadsheets
- **My Team** — Your team members and their activity
- **Companies** — Company profiles linked to your account
- **Approvals** — Jobs awaiting your approval

**For Recruiters** (sidebar items you will use daily):
- **Dashboard** — Overview of your sourcing activity
- **Mandates** — Jobs assigned to you for sourcing
- **Pipeline** — Candidates you are managing across stages
- **Candidates** — Candidate profiles you are working with
- **Candidate Bank** — Your searchable talent database
- **Referrals** — Candidate referrals you have submitted

## 2.4 Basic Daily Workflow

**Employer Daily Routine:**
1. Check Dashboard for new activity
2. Review any pending job approvals
3. Check Pipeline for candidate updates
4. Review Tracker for submission status changes
5. Take action on candidates (approve, reject, schedule interview)

**Recruiter Daily Routine:**
1. Check Dashboard for assigned mandates
2. Review Pipeline for candidate status updates
3. Add new candidates sourced during the day
4. Update candidate stages as progress is made
5. Check Tracker for submission tracking updates

## Module E3: Creating a Job Listing

**Purpose:** This is the most important employer task. Every hiring process starts with a well-created job.

**Time:** 20 minutes

**Learning Outcome:** You can create, configure, and publish a job listing.

### Steps to Perform

1. Click **Post Job** in the left sidebar (or navigate to My Jobs and click the "Create Job" button).
2. Fill in the required fields:

| Field | What to Enter | Example |
|-------|--------------|---------|
| **Title** | The job position name | "Production Manager" |
| **Description** | Detailed role description, responsibilities, requirements | Free-text, be thorough |
| **Company** | Select the company this job belongs to | Select from dropdown |
| **Location** | Where the job is based | "Gurgaon, Haryana" |
| **Job Type** | The employment type | Full-time, Part-time, Contract, or Remote |
| **Salary Min / Max** | Salary range (optional but recommended) | 800000 / 1200000 |
| **Experience Min / Max** | Years of experience required | 5 / 10 |
| **Public Company Alias** | The name candidates will see (can be masked) | "Leading Steel Manufacturer" |

3. Review all fields carefully.
4. Click **Submit** or **Create Job**.
5. The job may go to "Pending Approval" status depending on your portal settings. If so, an administrator will approve it before it goes live.

### Do This Now (Practice Exercise)

Create a test job with the following details:
- Title: "Test Engineer — Training Exercise"
- Location: "Delhi"
- Job Type: Full-time
- Experience: 3–5 years
- Description: "This is a training exercise job posting."

After creating it, verify it appears in your **My Jobs** list.

**Common Mistakes:**
- Leaving the description empty or writing only one line. A detailed description attracts better candidates.
- Forgetting to set the Public Company Alias. If left blank, the real company name may be visible to candidates when you want it masked.
- Setting salary as monthly when the field expects annual (or vice versa). Confirm the expected format with your administrator.

**Trainer Tip:** Create one job together as a group exercise, then have each trainee create their own. Walk around and check that all required fields are filled.

## Module E4: Editing a Job

**Purpose:** Jobs often need updates after creation — salary changes, description updates, or status changes.

**Time:** 10 minutes

**Learning Outcome:** You can find and edit any job you have created.

### Steps to Perform

1. Click **My Jobs** in the left sidebar.
2. Find the job you want to edit in the list.
3. Click on the job to open its detail view.

5. Make your changes to any field.
6. Click **Save** or **Update**.

### Job Status Transitions

Jobs can have the following statuses:

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Draft** | Job is saved but not yet submitted |
| **Pending Approval** | Job is submitted and waiting for admin approval |
| **Active** | Job is live and candidates can be added |
| **On Hold** | Job is temporarily paused |
| **Closed** | Hiring is complete, no new candidates |

**Trainer Tip:** Have each trainee edit the test job they created in Module E3. Ask them to change the location and add more detail to the description.

## Module E5: Understanding the Pipeline

**Purpose:** The pipeline is the heart of the portal. It shows where every candidate stands in the hiring process.

**Time:** 20 minutes

**Learning Outcome:** You can read the pipeline view and understand what each stage means.

### The 7 Pipeline Stages

Every candidate moves through these stages from left to right:

| # | Stage | What Happens Here |
|---|-------|-------------------|
| 1 | **Applied** | Candidate has been added or has applied to the job |
| 2 | **Shortlisted** | Recruiter has reviewed and shortlisted the candidate |
| 3 | **Submitted to Client** | Candidate profile has been submitted to the employer for review |
| 4 | **Interview** | Candidate is in the interview process |
| 5 | **Offered** | An offer has been extended to the candidate |
| 6 | **Hired** | Candidate has accepted the offer |
| 7 | **Joined** | Candidate has started working |

**Additional Statuses** (can happen at any stage):
- **Rejected** — Candidate was not selected (can happen at any stage)
- **On Hold** — Decision is paused (can happen at any stage)

### Steps to Perform

1. Click **Pipeline** in the left sidebar.
2. You will see a board or list view showing candidates grouped by stage.
3. Each candidate card shows:
   - Candidate name
   - Current stage
   - Job they are associated with
   - Date of last update
4. Click on any candidate to see their full profile.

### Do This Now (Practice Exercise)

Open the Pipeline view and answer these questions:
- How many candidates are currently in the "Interview" stage?
- Which job has the most candidates in "Submitted to Client"?
- Is anyone in "On Hold" status?

**Common Mistakes:**
- Confusing "Hired" with "Joined." Hired means the offer was accepted. Joined means the person has actually started work. Both must be updated.
- Not checking the Pipeline daily. Stale data leads to confusion with recruiters.

**Trainer Tip:** Project the Pipeline on screen and walk through 2–3 real candidates, explaining their journey from Applied to current stage.

## Module E6: Managing Applicants

**Purpose:** Taking action on candidates — approving, rejecting, or advancing them through stages.

**Time:** 15 minutes

**Learning Outcome:** You can review a candidate and move them to the next stage.

### Steps to Perform

1. From the Pipeline or from a Job detail view, click on a candidate.
2. Review the candidate's information:
   - Name, email, phone
   - Resume (download if available)
   - Current stage
   - Notes or comments from recruiters
3. To advance the candidate:
   - Look for a **Move to Next Stage** button or a stage selector dropdown.
   - Select the new stage (e.g., move from "Submitted to Client" to "Interview").
   - Confirm the action.
4. To reject a candidate:
   - Select **Rejected** from the stage options.
   - This removes them from the active pipeline but keeps their record.
5. To put a candidate on hold:
   - Select **On Hold** from the stage options.

**Common Mistakes:**
- Moving a candidate forward without reviewing their resume first.
- Forgetting to reject candidates you are not interested in. Leaving them in limbo confuses recruiters.
- Moving candidates backward in the pipeline (e.g., from Interview back to Applied). This is usually an error.

**Trainer Tip:** Use a real candidate example. Show: open profile, read resume, make a decision, move to next stage. Have each trainee do it once.

## Module E7: Using the Submission Tracker

**Purpose:** The Submission Tracker is a spreadsheet-style view that gives you a detailed, sortable overview of all candidate submissions across your jobs.

**Time:** 15 minutes

**Learning Outcome:** You can read, filter, and use the tracker to monitor hiring progress.

### Steps to Perform

1. Click **Trackers** in the left sidebar.
2. You will see a list of available trackers (your admin or recruiter may have created these).
3. Click on a tracker to open it.
4. The tracker opens as a grid/spreadsheet with columns such as:
   - Candidate Name
   - Job Title
   - Current Stage
   - Submission Date
   - Status
   - Notes
5. You can scroll horizontally to see all columns.
6. Click on any row to see more details about that candidate.

### Understanding Tracker vs. Pipeline

| Feature | Pipeline | Tracker |
|---------|----------|---------|
| **View Type** | Visual board (cards) | Spreadsheet (rows & columns) |
| **Best For** | Quick status overview | Detailed data review |
| **Actions** | Move candidates between stages | View and filter submission data |
| **Scope** | All candidates across jobs | Specific tracker scope |

**Common Mistake:** Thinking the Tracker and Pipeline are the same thing. They show the same data in different formats. Use Pipeline for quick actions, Tracker for detailed review.

**Trainer Tip:** Open a tracker and show how to read it column by column. Point out the most important columns first (Candidate Name, Stage, Date).

## Module R5: Moving Candidates Through Pipeline Stages

**Purpose:** As candidates progress in the hiring process, you must update their stage in the pipeline. This is your most frequent daily task.

**Time:** 20 minutes

**Learning Outcome:** You can move any candidate forward, backward, or to a parallel status (rejected/on hold) in the pipeline.

### The 7 Pipeline Stages (Recap)

```
Applied --> Shortlisted --> Submitted to Client --> Interview --> Offered --> Hired --> Joined
                                                      |
                                              [Rejected / On Hold]
                                        (can happen at any stage)
```

### Steps to Perform

1. Click **Pipeline** in the left sidebar.
2. Find the candidate you want to update.
3. Click on the candidate to open their profile.
4. Look for the **Stage** selector or **Move** button.
5. Select the new stage:
   - To advance: Select the next stage in order (e.g., "Shortlisted" to "Submitted to Client").
   - To reject: Select "Rejected."
   - To pause: Select "On Hold."
6. Confirm the change.
7. The candidate card will now appear in the new stage column.

### When to Move a Candidate

| Transition | When to Do It |
|-----------|--------------|
| Applied → Shortlisted | You have reviewed the resume and they are a potential fit |
| Shortlisted → Submitted to Client | You are sending this candidate to the employer for review |
| Submitted to Client → Interview | The employer wants to interview this candidate |
| Interview → Offered | The employer is extending an offer |
| Offered → Hired | The candidate has accepted the offer |
| Hired → Joined | The candidate has started work on their joining date |
| Any Stage → Rejected | The candidate is not moving forward |
| Any Stage → On Hold | A decision is pending |

### Real-World Example

You submitted "Rahul Sharma" to the "Plant Head" mandate two days ago. The employer calls and says they want to interview him next week. You would:
1. Open the Pipeline.
2. Find Rahul Sharma in the "Submitted to Client" column.
3. Click on his profile.
4. Move him to "Interview" stage.
5. The employer will see this update in their Pipeline view.

### Do This Now (Practice Exercise)

Find the test candidate you added in Module R4. Move them through these stages one at a time:
1. Applied → Shortlisted
2. Shortlisted → Submitted to Client
3. Submitted to Client → Interview

Verify the candidate appears in the correct stage after each move.

**Common Mistakes:**
- Skipping stages (moving directly from Applied to Interview). Always follow the stage order so tracking is accurate.
- Forgetting to update the stage after a phone call or email. If the employer told you to reject someone, update the system immediately.
- Leaving candidates in "Submitted to Client" for weeks. Follow up with the employer and update the stage.

**Trainer Tip:** This is the most critical module. Spend extra time here. Have each trainee move a candidate through 3 stages. Watch for errors.

## Module R8: Daily Workflow and Activity Updates

**Purpose:** Establish an efficient daily routine that keeps your pipeline accurate and your employers informed.

**Time:** 10 minutes

**Learning Outcome:** You have a repeatable daily process.

### Recommended Daily Schedule

| Time | Task | Where |
|------|------|-------|
| **9:00 AM** | Check Dashboard for new mandates | Dashboard |
| **9:15 AM** | Review Pipeline for overnight changes | Pipeline |
| **9:30 AM** | Follow up on "Submitted to Client" candidates | Pipeline |
| **10:00 AM – 4:00 PM** | Source and add new candidates | Candidate Bank |

| **4:30 PM** | Review Tracker for any missed updates | Tracker view |

### Quick Tips for Efficiency

1. **Update stages immediately** — Do not batch stage updates to end of day. Update as events happen.
2. **Add notes when moving candidates** — A brief note ("Employer liked profile, scheduling interview") helps everyone on the team.
3. **Check Candidate Bank before sourcing externally** — You may already have a matching candidate.
4. **Keep candidate contact info current** — A wrong phone number or email can cost you a placement.
5. **Do not leave candidates in limbo** — If there has been no activity for a week, follow up and update the stage.

# 5. ROLE COMPARISON TABLE

| Capability | Employer | Recruiter |
|-----------|----------|-----------|
| **Dashboard Access** | Employer Dashboard (hiring overview) | Recruiter Dashboard (sourcing overview) |
| **Create Jobs** | Yes (may require approval) | Yes (may require approval) |
| **Edit Jobs** | Yes (own jobs) | Yes (assigned jobs) |
| **Delete Jobs** | Yes (own jobs) | No |
| **View Pipeline** | Yes (own jobs and team) | Yes (assigned mandates) |
| **Move Candidates Between Stages** | Yes | Yes |
| **Add Candidates to Bank** | Via Candidate Bank | Via Candidate Bank |
| **Upload Resumes** | Yes | Yes |
| **View Submission Tracker** | Yes (all team trackers) | Yes (assigned trackers) |
| **Create Tracker Templates** | No | Yes |
| **Create Trackers** | No | Yes |
| **Manage Team** | View only | Not available |
| **View Companies** | Yes (assigned companies) | Not available |
| **Approve Jobs** | Yes (if approval workflow active) | No |
| **Referrals** | Not available | Yes |
| **View Analytics** | Yes (employer analytics) | Not available |

### Key Difference in Workflow

- **Employers** are decision-makers. They create jobs, review submissions, and approve or reject candidates.
- **Recruiters** are executors. They source candidates, submit them to jobs, and keep the pipeline moving.

Both roles rely on each other. Employers cannot hire without recruiter submissions. Recruiters cannot progress candidates without employer decisions.

# 6. TRAINER'S GUIDE

## 6.1 How to Run a 1-Day Training Session

### Audience
New employer or recruiter users who have never used the portal.

### Pre-Training Checklist

Before the training session:
- [ ] All trainee accounts are created and active.
- [ ] All trainees have received their login credentials via email.
- [ ] At least 2–3 test jobs exist in the system for practice.
- [ ] At least 5–10 test candidates exist in the Candidate Bank.
- [ ] Training room has stable internet and a projector/screen.

## 6.4 Common Onboarding Issues

| Issue | Solution |
|-------|----------|
| "I can't log in" | Verify the email matches what admin registered. Check for typos. Use "Forgot Password" if needed. |
| "I don't see any jobs" | Jobs must be created or assigned to the user. Check with admin that the user is assigned to the correct team/company. |
| "I can't create a job" | Verify the user has the correct role. Some roles may not have job creation permissions. |
| "My Pipeline is empty" | No candidates have been added to the user's jobs yet. This is normal for new accounts. |
| "I uploaded a resume but got an error" | Check file format (only PDF, DOC, DOCX) and file size (max 5MB). |
| "I moved a candidate to the wrong stage" | Contact admin to correct. In the future, double-check before confirming a stage change. |
| "I can't see my team" | Team visibility depends on the role. Recruiters do not have access to the My Team section. |
| "The Tracker looks different from what I expected" | Trackers can be customized. The columns and layout may vary. Ask admin which tracker to use. |

# 8. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

### General

**Q: Can I access the portal from my phone?**
A: The portal is designed for desktop/laptop browsers. While it may load on a mobile browser, we recommend using a computer for the best experience.

**Q: What browser should I use?**
A: Google Chrome (latest version) is recommended. Firefox and Edge also work. Avoid Internet Explorer.

**Q: What happens if I close my browser? Will I lose my work?**
A: Any saved data (jobs, candidates) is stored on the server. You will not lose saved work. However, unsaved form data will be lost. Always click Save/Submit before closing.

**Q: Can I undo a stage change?**
A: Stage changes are logged and generally should not be undone. If you made an error, contact your administrator to correct it.

### Employer Questions

**Q: Can I see which recruiter submitted a candidate?**
A: Yes. The candidate profile and tracker submissions show which recruiter submitted each candidate.

**Q: What does "Public Company Alias" mean?**
A: This is the company name that candidates see on the job listing. Use it when you want to keep the actual company name confidential. For example, instead of "JSW Steel," you might use "Leading Steel Manufacturer."

**Q: A job I created is still in "Pending Approval." What do I do?**
A: Your administrator needs to approve it. Contact them directly or wait. You can check the status in your My Jobs section.

**Q: Can I see resumes of all candidates?**
A: You can see resumes for candidates submitted to your jobs. You also have access to the Candidate Bank for browsing.

### Recruiter Questions

**Q: What is the difference between Candidate Bank and Pipeline?**
A: The **Candidate Bank** is your talent database — all candidates you have ever added. The **Pipeline** shows only candidates who are actively assigned to a job and moving through hiring stages.

**Q: Can I submit the same candidate to multiple jobs?**
A: Yes. A single candidate can be submitted to multiple mandates. They will appear separately in each job's pipeline.

**Q: What file formats can I upload for resumes?**
A: PDF, DOC, and DOCX only. Maximum file size is 5MB.

**Q: What should I do if I cannot find a mandate assigned to me?**
A: Check the Mandates page and verify all filters are cleared. If it is still missing, contact your administrator to confirm the assignment.

**Q: Can I delete a candidate I added by mistake?**
A: Generally, candidates cannot be deleted to maintain data integrity. Contact your administrator if a duplicate or test candidate needs to be removed.
