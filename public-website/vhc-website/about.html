About VHC Talent Advisory

Our Story

Founded over 25 years ago, VHC Talent Advisory has grown from a regional recruitment firm into a leading bi-continental talent partner serving businesses across India and the United States.

Our journey began with a simple yet powerful mission: to connect exceptional talent with organizations that value their potential. Today, we've placed thousands of professionals in leadership and specialist roles across diverse industries.

What sets us apart is our deep understanding of both Eastern and Western business cultures, enabling us to facilitate seamless cross-border talent acquisition and provide strategic advisory services that drive organizational success.
